<div class="page-content">

I'm Jacob Holtz. This is where I publish threat intelligence writeups —
malware analysis, actor tracking, and adversary tradecraft, usually mapped
to [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/) where relevant.

Each writeup carries a [Traffic Light Protocol](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/news/traffic-light-protocol-tlp-definitions-and-usage)
marking (`TLP:CLEAR`, `TLP:GREEN`, `TLP:AMBER`, or `TLP:RED`) indicating how
freely it can be shared. Anything published here is `TLP:CLEAR` by default —
safe for unrestricted sharing — since it's on a public blog.

Reach me on [GitHub]({{ 'https://github.com/' | append: site.author.github }}).
